You will receive a structured dataset describing a full round of an Among Us game in Minecraft.

Your goal is to analyze the entire round and produce two summaries.

====================
GAME DATA
====================

PLAYERS (alive):
${alivePlayers}

PLAYERS (dead):
${deadPlayers}

ROLES:
${roles}

GAME DURATION:
${duration}

WINNER:
${winner}

TIMELINE OF EVENTS (chronological):
${timeline}

====================
TASK
====================

Create two summaries based ONLY on the provided data.

1. SHORT SUMMARY
- 1–2 sentences
- Focus on key turning points and the final outcome

2. LONG SUMMARY
- 4–6 sentences
- Describe the flow of the game from start to finish
- Include:
  - important kills
  - meetings and why they were called
  - voting behavior and key votes
  - eliminations (who got ejected and why if visible)
  - how the game ended

Format:
```
SHORT SUMMARY:
<short summary>

LONG SUMMARY:
<long summary>
```

====================
RULES
====================

- Do NOT invent or assume any information
- Only use what is explicitly provided
- Keep the tone neutral and clear
- Be concise but informative
- Do NOT use Markdown formatting
